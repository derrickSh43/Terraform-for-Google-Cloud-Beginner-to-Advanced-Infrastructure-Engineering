# Lesson 01 Challenge

Meridian's security lead has three new asks. Extend your lab solution. Exact
syntax is up to you, and the provider docs are your reference.

```powershell
python course.py check 01 --challenge
```

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| C1.1 | Another team already owns `meridian-dev-4821-assets`. Make the bucket name unique by adding a random suffix generated by the **`hashicorp/random`** provider. Declare and pin it like the Google provider. `init` will fetch it from the Registry. |
| C1.2 | Versioning keeps every overwrite forever, which gets expensive. Delete **noncurrent** object versions after at most 90 days. |
| C1.3 | Labels will soon be shared by many resources. Define them once as a local value and reference it. |
| C1.4 | Finance wants predictable costs: the bucket must be `STANDARD` storage in your single region, not a multi-region. |

### Think about it

- `random_id.hex` is **unknown until apply**. Look at the plan: what does Terraform show for the bucket name?
- Once applied, why won't the random suffix change on every plan? (Hint: state.)
